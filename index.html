Thirupathi

Tirumala Temple, also known as Sri Venkateswara Temple, is a famous Hindu shrine located in Tirumala, Andhra Pradesh. Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara (a form of Vishnu), it is one of the richest and most visited temples in the world. Set atop the Seven Hills, the temple is known for its spiritual significance, grand festivals, and ancient Dravidian architecture. Devotees visit from across the globe to seek blessings and offer prayers.

Varanasi

Varanasi, also known as Kashi or Banaras, is one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities—dating back over 3,000 years—and has long been a major center of Vedic religion, philosophy, education, music, and trade, especially in silk, muslin, perfumes, and brassware . Situated on the holy Ganges River, it is India’s most sacred Hindu pilgrimage site: devotees bathe in the river to wash away sins, and many believe that dying here grants moksha, or liberation from the cycle of rebirth . Daily life in Varanasi centers around its iconic ghats—most famously Dashashwamedh Ghat, where the evening Ganga Aarti is performed, and the cremation ghats of Manikarnika and Harishchandra, where funeral rites are carried out—melding rituals of life and death into the city’s spiritual fabric . The city's cultural landscape includes the world‑famous Kashi Vishwanath Temple dedicated to Shiva, the intellectual and artistic legacy of Banaras Hindu University, and the tradition of Banarasi silk weaving, music, literature, and classical arts that continue to thrive here .

Yadadri

Yadadri, formerly known as Yadagirigutta, is a sacred hilltop temple town in Telangana, about 60 km from Hyderabad. It is home to the renowned Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple, where Lord Narasimha is believed to have appeared in five forms. The temple has undergone a major transformation in recent years, featuring stunning Dravidian-style architecture, intricately carved stone structures, and a golden Sudarshana Chakra atop its sanctum. Yadadri is a popular pilgrimage site, often called the "Telangana Tirupati," attracting devotees year-round for its spiritual significance and majestic beauty.
